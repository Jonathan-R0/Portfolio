## Platform

## Users

Recruiters and technical teams evaluating Jonathan's software engineering work. Confirmed by the owner during the redesign.

## Product Purpose

Present Jonathan Rosenblatt's real projects, computing background, and writing, with clear routes to his resume and contact details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, static export for GitHub Pages. Preserve the home, projects, about, blog, and article routes and existing factual content and external destinations. All-site redesign approved by the owner. No invented employment, clients, achievements, or availability claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Project descriptions and links in public/projects.json; article content in public/blogs.json; existing portrait at public/profileimage.jpg and project images at public/img/. Existing resume hosted on Google Drive. GitHub, LinkedIn, and email destinations are in the incumbent components.

## Brand Commitments

Jonathan David Rosenblatt; backend development; Computer Engineering at FIUBA; Argentina. The owner rejects the incumbent palette and generic visual decoration and authorizes a replacement visual system.

## Product Principles

- Let actual engineering work provide the evidence.
- Make project source, resume, and contact easy to find.
- Preserve readable content and direct navigation on mobile and desktop.
